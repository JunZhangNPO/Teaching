## Setup

Six to ten participants stand in a circle. The facilitator provides several soft stress balls.

## Activity

### Round 1: Learn Names Together

One participant tosses a ball to another person. Before each toss, the whole group says the recipient’s name together. Continue until everyone has had opportunities to receive the ball and hear the names.

### Round 2: Practice Individual Communication

Use one ball again. This time, only the person tossing the ball says the recipient’s name. The sender checks that the recipient is ready before tossing.

### Round 3: Coordinate with Multiple Balls

Introduce additional balls so several exchanges happen simultaneously. Participants continue calling the recipient’s name and checking readiness while coordinating multiple exchanges.

### Pause: Reflect and Give Feedback

Stop the activity and discuss:

- What worked well?
- What became difficult with multiple balls?
- What do you need others to do differently?

Encourage specific requests, such as:

- “Please toss the ball lower.”
- “Please check that I’m ready.”
- “Let’s slow down the pace.”

### Repeat: Apply Feedback and Improve

Repeat the multiple-ball activity using the group’s suggestions. Pause afterward to discuss which adjustments helped and what still needs improvement.

## Pedagogical Reflection

This activity follows a cycle of **practice, reflection, feedback, and improvement**. Participants learn names, communicate intentions, express their needs, and adjust their behavior in response to others.

The increasing complexity makes coordination challenges visible. Repeating the activity allows participants to test their suggestions and observe how feedback can improve group performance.
