# Teaching in the AI Era: An Instructor’s Perspective

Higher education today faces a convergence of structural pressures:
- Declining enrollment
- Growing skepticism toward the value of university degrees
- The rapid integration of generative AI into student workflows (most visibly in coursework and assignments)

Rather than treating AI solely as an academic integrity threat, instructors must rethink the core value proposition of a university classroom.

## 1. Shifting What We Offer: Research-Led and Experience-Driven Instruction

Foundational textbook knowledge is now easily accessible.
Students can independently retrieve and synthesize introductory concepts, often through faster or more personalized tools than traditional lectures provide.

Consequently, instructors must move beyond delivering standardized content.
Instead, we should anchor our teaching in:
- **Research expertise**
- **Lived experience**
- **Tacit domain knowledge**

By tailoring foundational concepts to students' specific needs and demonstrating how scholars actually navigate complex, ambiguous problems, the classroom offers irreplaceable intellectual value that static textbooks and generic AI outputs cannot replicate.

## 2. Prioritizing In-Person Dialogue, Debate, and Intellectual Challenge

While AI can simulate arguments, it cannot replace the friction and growth that come from encountering genuine, lived human perspectives.

**In-person group activities, structured discussions, and classroom debates** are more essential than ever.

Bringing students together to:
- Articulate their reasoning
- Listen across differences
- Challenge one another's assumptions

This process cultivates critical thinking and intellectual resilience in ways solitary interaction with an AI never could.

## 3. Bridging Theory and Practice Through Community Engagement

To counter doubts about the practical relevance of higher education, courses should actively connect classroom learning to real-world contexts.

**Community engagement and applied projects** enable students to:
- Test theoretical frameworks in practice
- Collaborate with external stakeholders
- Develop transferable skills

Grounding academic inquiry in tangible community needs reinforces the civic and professional value of a university degree.

## 4. Cultivating AI Literacy and Ethical Practice Through Mutual Learning

Because students inevitably use AI in diverse ways, instructors bear a responsibility to guide them toward **effective, critical, and ethical AI usage**.

Doing so requires instructors to stay informed about evolving AI capabilities and invest in their own AI literacy first—combining domain expertise with an understanding of how these tools succeed and fail.

Crucially, developing AI literacy should be framed as an open, **mutual learning process** between instructors and students:

* **Transparency and Shared Examples:**
  Instructors and students should openly communicate about how they use AI in their workflows.
  Sharing concrete examples of productive versus problematic applications helps build shared norms.

* **Accountability and Verification:**
  In classroom debates, for instance, students may be permitted to use AI during preparation, with the explicit caveat that the opposing team has access to the exact same tools.
  Therefore, each team remains fully responsible for:
  - Verifying facts
  - Evaluating sources
  - Ensuring the accuracy of their arguments
  
  Students must recognize that unverified AI-generated claims will be dismantled under peer scrutiny.

## 5. Redesigning Assessments Around Oral and Collaborative Demonstration

As take-home essays and standard written assignments become increasingly vulnerable to AI substitution, assessment strategies must evolve to evaluate genuine comprehension and real-time reasoning.

Prioritizing **oral exams, structured group discussions, and live presentations** shifts the focus from static written products to dynamic intellectual engagement.

This approach ensures that students can:
- Defend their ideas
- Respond to follow-up questions
- Demonstrate authentic mastery
